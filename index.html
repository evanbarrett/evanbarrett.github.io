I am a PhD pre-candidate at the University of Michigan's Department of Climate and Space Sciences.

My current research in Atmospheric Sciences is advised by professors Claire Pettersen and Adriana Bailey.

I hold a bachelor's in Earth Sciences and Mathematics from Dartmouth College, and did my undergraduate research focused on paleoclimate in the Tropics and snow surface processes in the context of local meteorology at Summit Station, Greenland.
